# NOVA SKIN — Product

**What it is:** a portfolio Shopify store (custom Online Store 2.0 theme) for a fictional premium skincare brand. All brand, product and content details are sample data.

**Who it's for**
- *Shopper:* 25–45, buys skincare by concern (hydration, brightening, anti-ageing, sensitive), reads ingredients, shops on a phone.
- *Merchant:* edits every word, image, product pick and section order in the Theme Editor without touching code.
- *Hiring reviewer:* reads the repo to judge Liquid, schema, Ajax cart, performance and accessibility skill.

**What must stay true**
1. Mobile-first. Every flow (browse → filter → product → drawer → checkout) works one-handed at 390px.
2. Merchant-editable. Client facts live in schema settings, metafields, menus and locales, never hard-coded in Liquid.
3. Honest. No invented reviews, stats, press or certifications. Testimonials are marked "Sample", and reviews come only from the review app as labelled test data.
4. Works without JS. Add to cart, filters and the cart page all fall back to standard form posts.
5. Fast and accessible. Lighthouse mobile ≥ 90, WCAG AA contrast, keyboard-operable drawer and menus, reduced-motion respected.
6. Only the apps that are needed (Search & Discovery, Judge.me, Shopify Email). No app installed just to look busy.

**Primary actions:** Shop by concern · Add to cart · Subscribe to the newsletter.
